## 🚀 1. Overview & Core Mission
**ARTEMIS PRISM** is an independent, physics-informed lunar mission planning and digital-twin web application designed to evaluate lunar south-pole landing sites and exploration scenarios with mathematical rigor and absolute scientific integrity.

The system integrates:
- **Lunar Elevation & Topography:** LOLA 5 m/px high-resolution DEM and SLDEM2015 60 m/px regional mosaic.
- **Slope & Geomorphology:** Horn’s 3×3 finite-difference slope across 5m, 10m, and 20m baselines, plus 95th-percentile lander footprint hazard screening.
- **Surface Roughness:** Terrain Ruggedness Index (TRI) and Topographic Position Index (TPI).
- **Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSR):** Lemelin et al. (2021) georeferenced database and cold-trap proximity analysis.
- **Ephemerides & Solar Geometry:** NASA JPL Horizons DE421 topocentric Sun and Earth vectors.
- **Dynamic Terrain Shadows:** 64-sector horizon elevation profile matrices incorporating lunar spherical curvature ($R_M = 1737.4\text{ km}$) and partial solar disk occultation ($0.533^\circ$).
- **Direct Earth Visibility & Telecom:** Lunar libration tracking ($\pm 6.68^\circ$ lat, $\pm 7.92^\circ$ lon) and line-of-sight margins.
- **Thermal Environment Model:** Radiative equilibrium proxy ($T \sim 39\text{ K}$ in PSRs to $\sim 228\text{ K}$ on sunlit ridges) calibrated against LRO Diviner radiometer data.
- **Power & Energy Storage:** Solar panel orientation (vertical tracking vs horizontal), dust degradation, and battery State-of-Charge (SOC %) simulation with a 20% critical floor.
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA):** Deterministic TOPSIS, Weighted Sum Model (WSM), hard-constraint vetoes, and exact Shapley value attributions.
- **Rover Traverse Feasibility:** Sun-Chaser time-expanded $A^*$ search routing over continuous terrain and dynamic lighting.
- **Mission Replay & Survival Clock:** Interactive timeline scrubbing across the 2026 Artemis III synodic illumination epoch.

## 🛡️ 2. Mandatory Scientific Integrity Rules (Rules 1–14)
ARTEMIS PRISM enforces the following principles across all modules and in its integrated **MoonByte AI**:
1. **Never fabricate scientific data:** Zero synthetic elevations, fake coordinates, or fabricated NASA measurements. Missing values are explicitly stated as "Data unavailable" or "Not computed".
2. **Four-tier Information Categorization:**
   - 🟢 **Observed or published data:** Directly from peer-reviewed databases (LOLA, Diviner, Lemelin et al., Horizons).
   - 🔵 **Computed result:** Deterministically calculated by ARTEMIS PRISM using documented physics equations.
   - 🟡 **Modelled estimate:** Generated by an analytical model with stated assumptions (e.g. radiative thermal proxy).
   - 🟣 **Illustrative example:** Demonstrative scenario clearly flagged as non-flight-certified.
3. **No False Safety Guarantees:** Sites are evaluated as "candidate", "screened", or "vetoed" based on stated thresholds. No site is declared unconditionally "safe" or "flight-certified".
4. **Binding PSR Coverage Constraint:** The $16.42\%$ PSR coverage metric is strictly bound to the $85.0^\circ\text{S} - 90.0^\circ\text{S}$ study domain ($31,415.9\text{ km}^2$) and never generalized to the entire Moon.

## 🧮 3. Scientific Mathematical Formulations

### 3.1 Horn’s 3×3 Finite-Difference Slope
Given a $3 \times 3$ elevation window with cell size $\Delta x, \Delta y$:
$$\left[ \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right] = \frac{(z_{TR} + 2z_R + z_{BR}) - (z_{TL} + 2z_L + z_{BL})}{8 \Delta x}$$
$$\left[ \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right] = \frac{(z_{BL} + 2z_B + z_{BR}) - (z_{TL} + 2z_T + z_{TR})}{8 \Delta y}$$
$$\text{Slope}^\circ = \arctan\left(\sqrt{\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2}\right) \times \frac{180}{\pi}$$

### 3.2 Lunar Curvature Horizon Raymarch
At distance $d$ from the observer, apparent terrain drop due to lunar curvature ($R_{\text{Moon}} = 1,737,400\text{ m}$):
$$\Delta h_{\text{curv}} = \frac{d^2}{2 R_{\text{Moon}}}$$
$$\theta_{\text{horiz}}(\alpha) = \max_d \left( \arctan\left(\frac{z(d) - \Delta h_{\text{curv}} - z_{\text{obs}}}{d}\right) \right)$$

### 3.3 Radiative Equilibrium Thermal Proxy
$$T_{\text{surf}} \approx \left( \frac{S_0 (1 - A) \sin(\theta_{\text{sun}}) \cdot f_{\text{sunlit}} + q_{\text{geo}}}{\varepsilon \sigma} \right)^{1/4}$$
Where:
- $S_0 = 1361.0\text{ W/m}^2$ (solar irradiance)
- $A = 0.12$ (lunar regolith Bond albedo)
- $\varepsilon = 0.95$ (infrared emissivity)
- $\sigma = 5.67037 \times 10^{-8}\text{ W/(m}^2\text{K}^4\text{)}$
- $q_{\text{geo}} = 0.018\text{ W/m}^2$ (geothermal flux)
